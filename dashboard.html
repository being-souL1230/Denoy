Privacy & Data Architecture

Privacy & Data Architecture

Your research stays private.
Audit records remain transparent.

DarkSpot operates on a local-first, zero-telemetry architecture designed for students, job applicants, and researchers verifying high-stakes circulars. We require zero user accounts, zero passwords, and zero personal tracking cookies.

1. Zero User Accounts, Zero Logins, and Zero Tracking Cookies

You never need to create an account, provide an email address, or disclose personal identity to use DarkSpot. We do not set analytics trackers, advertising beacons, or surveillance cookies in your browser. Every investigation session runs in complete anonymity.

2. What Stays Strictly on Your Device (Local Memory and Storage)

Your research goals, candidate webpage URLs, pasted notice paragraphs, and generated inspection matrices reside strictly within your active browser session memory (localStorage and transient memory). DarkSpot does not maintain persistent database logs of your search history, and no third-party analytics tool monitors your queries.

3. AI Synthesis and Cloud Processing (Groq API)

When you initiate an investigation, the system transmits only the sanitized research question and extracted circular paragraphs to the Groq Cloud API for criteria extraction and contradiction synthesis. No user identifiers, device fingerprints, or IP addresses are attached to these calls. Under standard API developer terms, prompts are processed transiently and are never retained to train frontier models.

4. Verifiable Quote Integrity (Zero Hallucination Guarantee)

DarkSpot never generates fabricated claims. Every single quote cited in the Coverage Matrix, Conflict Dossier, and Action Checklist is verified character-by-character against original ingested source text by deterministic verification code before being presented to you.

5. Full Data Control, Instant Reset, and Offline Export

You retain complete sovereignty over your research dossier. You can export your full findings at any moment as a portable JSON file. Closing or refreshing the page purges runtime analysis state immediately. You can also wipe active data at any point.

Local session isolation is active in this browser. No personal research queries or document text are ever stored in persistent cloud databases.
